src/detect.js ports the classical cell candidate detection of cellfinder 1.10.1
(cellfinder/core/detect: plane filters, tile walker, ball filter, structure detection and
structure splitting; https://github.com/brainglobe/cellfinder),
Copyright (c) 2020, Adam Tyson, University College London, under the BSD 3-Clause licence
(LICENSE-cellfinder).

The orientation notation and the registration outputs follow brainreg
(https://github.com/brainglobe/brainreg, BSD-3-Clause); no brainreg code is included.

Registration runs Greedy through @neurodesk/greedy (Apache-2.0; see packages/greedy/NOTICE).

The Allen Mouse Brain Common Coordinate Framework v3, (c) 2015-2017 Allen Institute for Brain
Science, is not part of this source. The web app downloads it at run time from the Neurodesk
dataset on Hugging Face, and the offline (Standalone) bundles include it in their asset
inventories. It is redistributed for research and noncommercial use only, under the Allen
Institute terms of use (https://alleninstitute.org/terms-of-use/). Commercial use, including
commercial redistribution of an offline bundle that contains it, needs the Allen Institute's
prior written permission.

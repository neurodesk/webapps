Input image

Prepared T1c on the 240 × 240 × 155 BraTS/SRI24 grid. iAURORA expects a skull-stripped, contrast-enhanced T1 (T1c) NIfTI registered to the 1 mm SRI24/BraTS atlas grid (240 × 240 × 155). BrainLes preprocessing or the BraTS orchestrator produce this grid. Multi-modality combinations and test-time augmentation are available in the Standalone container.

Metastasis segmentation

Model: T1c-only (t1c-o_best, deterministic baseline).

Advanced settings

Automatic uses the GPU through WebGPU when available.

Output

Drop a prepared T1c NIfTI anywhere, or choose an example.

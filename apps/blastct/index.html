Input scan

3D non-contrast head CT in Hounsfield Units (NIfTI or DICOM). iBLAST-CT automatically reorients and resamples any 3D non-contrast head CT to 1 mm isotropic, clamps Hounsfield Units to [-15, 100], segments four traumatic brain injury lesion classes (IPH, EAH, oedema, IVH), and maps labels back to native geometry. Volumes exceeding 32 million voxels at 1 mm (or 150 million native voxels) are refused before allocation.

Lesion segmentation

Model: DeepMedic v2 (checkpoint 1, 4 lesion classes).

Advanced settings

Automatic uses WebGPU when a hardware GPU is available.

Output

Drop a 3D head CT scan anywhere, or choose an example.

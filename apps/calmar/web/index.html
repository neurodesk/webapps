Lesion size: what the evidence says

i The lesion's volume, read before its location: larger lesions damage more regions and match more findings whatever their position, and volume is itself one of the stronger correlates of severity. Group associations, not predictions.

Lesion location: what the evidence says

i Published group-level findings about symptoms, matched to the regions this lesion damages through the aphasia knowledge base's curated atlas mapping. Literature associations, not predictions for this person.

White-matter disconnection: what the evidence says

i JHU reference-tract overlap and, when available, ENIGMA streamline-based disconnection with linked knowledge-base findings. Atlas overlap does not measure the fraction of fibres disconnected.

Therapy evidence for the implicated impairments

i Therapies whose knowledge-base entries target the symptom-level impairments the lesion location points to. Research associations from the lesion alone, not prescriptions.

About CALMaR

CALMaR

Co-designed Automated Lesion Mapping and Reporting.

This app loads a structural T1 (or a manual MNI-aligned lesion mask) locally, runs the CALMaR lesion mapping chain in the browser, and never uploads patient data.

The pipeline: SynthStrip brain extraction → SynthStroke baseline lesion segmentation → SynthMorph deformable registration to MNI152 (1603 1mm) → affine-aware resample to the selected atlas grid → atlas overlap → group-FC weighted-sum → threshold + cluster cleanup. All stages are exposed as individual buttons under Advanced controls; Run analysis chains them in one click.

Background execution is possible if this site is added under "Always keep these sites active" in your browser settings.

Privacy

Your data remains on your device.

Uploaded images and masks are read into browser memory for local viewing and analysis.

What this means

  • No patient image data, lesion masks, behavioural observations, assessment scores, voxel values, screenshots, or generated outputs are uploaded by the app.
  • Atlas and model assets may be fetched by the browser when a workflow needs them.
  • Downloaded result files are created locally from browser memory.

Third-party services

Static app files and public atlas assets may be served from external hosting, but patient-derived files are not sent to those services by the analysis code. The Neurodesk hosting layer records page views only, unless your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. It does not send custom events, patient images, masks, voxel values, screenshots, generated outputs, filenames, or analysis results to Google Analytics.

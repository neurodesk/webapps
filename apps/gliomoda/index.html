Input images

Skull-stripped, on the 240 × 240 × 155 BraTS/SRI24 grid. iGlioMODA was trained on BraTS images: co-registered, skull-stripped and resampled to the SRI24 atlas at 1 mm. BrainLesion's preprocessing package or the BraTS orchestrator produce them. GlioMODA has models for 11 combinations of the four sequences; the sidebar names the one it will use.

Tumour segmentation

Load at least one of T1c, FLAIR, T1 or T2.

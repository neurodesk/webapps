Each SciGet catalogue now has its own offline desktop suite. MSK Webapps, Microscopy Webapps and Preclinical Webapps hold their catalogue's apps, carry its title and logo on the offline start page, and install as `msk-webapps`, `microscopy-webapps` and `preclinical-webapps`. They release nightly as `webapps-<catalogue>-vVERSION` beside the full Neurodesk Webapps suite, which keeps every app, every DICOM View handoff and the Apptainer image. Once an edition is published, its catalogue's Standalone button links it instead of the full suite.

Compute double connective-field profiles as MATLAB R2023b's `corr(Y, X)`: both courses centred with MATLAB's `mean`, `y' * X` summed in the order MKL's `dgemv` uses on AMD hosts, then divided by `norm(y)` and each seed's norm and limited to [-1, 1]. The port took a dot product of unit-norm courses instead, a last-bit difference in most values. A pre-registered recording fingerprinted the order of double `y' * A`: columns in full blocks of four sum in two lanes, the remaining columns in the trees `?ormqr` uses at their position. That model reproduced every held-out fingerprint and random product on the AMD host, and now every unsmoothed double CF profile of the pinned reverse-correlation recordings equals MATLAB's on its AMD hosts (26 cases, 4,914 values). The same recording confirms that MATLAB, like the port since this release, turns a constant non-zero raw course into rounding noise with unit variance, and it rejects a candidate formula for `gampdf` below shape 1, which keeps its closed form. Smoothed profiles are built from these and are not checked bit for bit. The global signal's across-vertex order, CF Gaussian fits, convex-hull centroids and every Intel host still differ from MATLAB; the public single RC 2D and CF Gaussian refusals stay.

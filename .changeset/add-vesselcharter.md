Add VesselCharter (`vesselcharter`) and `@neurodesk/vesselcharter` for browser-native and standalone CLI 3D vascular morphometry on binary NIfTI vessel masks (including VesselBoost segmentations): exact anisotropic 3D EDT, clean-room Lee–Kashyap–Chu (1994) thinning, loop-preserving vascular graph extraction, clamped Taubin smoothing, subvoxel inscribed-ellipsoid and Voronoi volume-equivalent diameters, DM/SOAM tortuosity, bifurcation daughter angles and Murray ratios, optional brain/ROI vascular density, and deterministic JSON, CSV, NIfTI, and 3D MZ3 exports.

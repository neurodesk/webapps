SDC adds SyN-SDC to its method selector: sdcflows 2.17.1's fieldmap-less correction from one DWI series and a T1w (`@neurodesk/sdc/syn`), run in a worker in the browser. MindGrab masks the mean b0 and the T1w on its CPU modules unless masks are uploaded. The sidebar asks for the phase-encoding direction and the total readout time only when no sidecar has them, and a run without them is refused. The app writes the corrected DWI series with its bval, bvec and sidecar, the field map in Hz, its B-spline coefficients, the displacement field and the brain mask, shows the field over the corrected mean b0, and logs per-stage times and field QC in the console. The new example is the ds005134 slab with the full T1w; its outputs equal the Node pipeline's byte for byte. `createNiivueVolumePreview` draws an optional signed overlay on its image and clears the viewer when given `null`. The SyN-SDC N4 worker (`@neurodesk/sdc/syn/n4-worker`) takes the kernel's `wasm` URL as optional: without it the module loads the `n4-bias.wasm` beside it, so the composite site serves one N4 binary to N4 and SDC (runtime family `n4-wasm`). SDC stages only MindGrab's CPU modules, the only ones it runs. A new file-input kind, `mask`, marks an optional mask beside a scan input: the shell's page drop never fills it and never makes it the primary input. SDC's two mask fields use it, carry visible labels and are disabled while PyHySCO is selected, so a dropped DWI series stays with its T1w and a dropped AP/PA pair stays together. A flat BIDS-named DWI with a T1w that has no BIDS name (or a DICOM T1w) is read as loose files.

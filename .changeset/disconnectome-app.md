New app: Disconnectome scores which white-matter bundles a lesion disconnects, intersecting the lesion with the 87-bundle HCP1065 atlas in WebAssembly and drawing the damaged bundles coloured by a viridis ramp over their damage. The downloadable TSV is byte-identical to the nii2tvx command-line tool.

MuscleMap, SeedSeg and Carotid Flow move to the SciGet MSK catalogue at https://mskapps.sciget.org/. Links to their old addresses on webapps.neurodesk.org redirect there with the rest of the path, the query and the fragment. Their app bar, page title and privacy text name SciGet.

DICOM View on webapps.neurodesk.org no longer offers MuscleMap, SeedSeg or Carotid Flow under "Open in another webapp", because a handoff only works within one site. These apps still open DICOM and NIfTI files directly, and the desktop suite keeps every handoff.

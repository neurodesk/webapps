Add micro-sam, interactive 2D segmentation of light-microscopy images with Segment Anything for Microscopy (`vit_t_lm`). Positive and negative clicks and boxes segment one object at a time; accepted objects form a label TIFF and a per-object CSV table. On the BBBC038 example the browser gives micro-sam's PyTorch masks byte for byte for 24 fixed prompts.

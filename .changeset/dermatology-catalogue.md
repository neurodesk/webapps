Add the Dermatology Webapps catalogue (`dermatologyapps.sciget.org`) with five browser-native dermatology imaging applications and pinned open-licensed examples:

- **Dermoscope ABCD** (`dermoscope`): DullRazor hair removal, Shades of Gray color constancy, vignette masking, Otsu lesion segmentation, and experimental ABCD morphometrics.
- **Skin Chromophore** (`skin-chromophore`): sRGB-to-CIELAB colorimetry, Individual Typology Angle (ITA°) typology mapping, RGB erythema/melanin optical-density proxies, and Target-vs-Reference CIEDE2000 analysis.
- **Wound Planimetry** (`wound-planimetry`): interactive two-point ruler calibration, wound bed segmentation, oriented length × width planimetry, and color-based RYB tissue estimates.
- **DermCompare** (`derm-compare`): multi-scale rigid sequential dermoscopy registration with Curtain, Flicker, Checkerboard, and CIEDE2000 change views.
- **DermViT** (`dermvit`): local 7-class HAM10000 Vision Transformer classification (`onnxruntime-web`) with optional Shades of Gray normalization and occlusion sensitivity maps.

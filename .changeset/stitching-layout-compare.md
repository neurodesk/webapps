Show what stitching changed. The viewer's Layout and Stitched views put the layout preview and the stitched mosaic on one grid with one contrast window, and switching between them keeps the zoom, so a seam that is doubled in Layout can be checked in Stitched. Tile outlines mark every tile at its layout position (cyan) and where stitching placed it (orange); an Outlines button hides them. The wheel now zooms at the pointer and right-drag pans, and the status line reports how far tiles moved.

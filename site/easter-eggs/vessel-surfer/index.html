Controls

Move the mouse away from the centre ring to turn that way; the further out, the faster the turn. Or steer with WASD / arrow keys. On a phone, drag anywhere to steer. R or the Turn button flips you around, Shift or Stop brakes, B or Back reverses, and Space pauses. Change speed any time with the slider, + / − or the mouse wheel. The sub gently follows the vessel; aim into an opening to take a branch. Facing a wall stops you so you can turn away without a bump.
